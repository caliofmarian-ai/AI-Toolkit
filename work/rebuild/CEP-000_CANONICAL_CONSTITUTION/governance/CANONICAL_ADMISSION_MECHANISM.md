# CANONICAL ADMISSION MECHANISM

## Constitutional Authority

This mechanism is derived from the Human-Accepted CEP-000 Canonical Constitution.

Its principal constitutional authorities are Articles 18 through 24.

This mechanism does not itself admit any candidate into Canon.

It defines the governed path through which Canonical Admission may legitimately occur.

---

# 1. Fundamental Rule

Nothing becomes Canon merely because it:

- exists;
- is implemented;
- passes tests;
- is Production-Ready;
- is stored in Git;
- is placed inside the canonical destination;
- is generated by AI;
- is recommended by an audit;
- or is accepted implicitly.

Canonical Admission requires explicit evidence, explicit state, and explicit legitimate Human Authority.

No AI, script, test, audit, implementation, or repository operation may independently complete the transition to CANON.

---

# 2. Canonical Admission Identity

Every admission proceeding must identify exactly one canonical candidate.

The proceeding must record:

- Candidate ID;
- Candidate title;
- Candidate version or immutable Git identity when applicable;
- Candidate source location;
- Candidate current governance state;
- Candidate intended canonical destination;
- Human Authority;
- Admission evidence location.

Ambiguous candidate identity blocks admission.

---

# 3. Mandatory Five-Dimension Examination

Before a candidate may reach the Human Canonical Admission Gate, all five constitutional dimensions must be examined.

## 3.1 Canonical Identity

The examination must establish what the candidate is.

Result:

- PASS;
- GAP;
- or REVIEW.

## 3.2 Canonical Meaning

The examination must establish what the candidate means.

Result:

- PASS;
- GAP;
- or REVIEW.

## 3.3 Canonical Purpose

The examination must establish why the candidate exists within the organism.

Result:

- PASS;
- GAP;
- or REVIEW.

## 3.4 Canonical Relations

The examination must establish how the candidate relates to the organism and relevant canonical entities.

Result:

- PASS;
- GAP;
- or REVIEW.

## 3.5 Canonical Justification

The examination must establish why the candidate deserves canonical authority.

Result:

- PASS;
- GAP;
- or REVIEW.

A candidate cannot reach the Human Canonical Admission Gate while any mandatory dimension remains GAP or REVIEW.

---

# 4. Governance States

The permitted constitutional states are:

## RESEARCH

Material under investigation without Canonical Authority.

## CANDIDATE

Material formally proposed for Canonical Admission.

## REVIEW

Material undergoing canonical examination.

## CANON

Material explicitly admitted into Canon by legitimate Human Authority.

## CONTESTED

Existing Canon under formal reevaluation because serious contradictory Evidence exists.

## SUPERSEDED

Previously canonical material legitimately replaced while remaining historically traceable.

## REJECTED

Material formally examined and rejected for Canonical Admission.

No implementation-specific hidden state may silently substitute for these constitutional states.

---

# 5. Permitted State Transitions

The ordinary admission path is:

RESEARCH
→ CANDIDATE
→ REVIEW
→ HUMAN CANONICAL ADMISSION GATE
→ CANON

A candidate may instead move from REVIEW to:

- CANDIDATE, when more work is required;
- REJECTED, when legitimate Human Authority rejects admission.

Existing CANON may move to:

CANON
→ CONTESTED
→ REVIEW

and, after legitimate Human judgment, may:

- return to CANON;
- become SUPERSEDED;
- or be replaced by newly admitted CANON.

No automated process may independently execute a transition into CANON.

---

# 6. Human Canonical Admission Gate

The Human Canonical Admission Gate may be reached only when:

1. Candidate identity is exact.
2. Canonical Identity = PASS.
3. Canonical Meaning = PASS.
4. Canonical Purpose = PASS.
5. Canonical Relations = PASS.
6. Canonical Justification = PASS.
7. No unresolved admission GAP remains.
8. No unresolved admission REVIEW remains.
9. Intended canonical destination is explicit.
10. Admission evidence is conserved.
11. The candidate presented to Human Authority is the same candidate that was examined.

At the gate, Human Authority may explicitly:

- ADMIT;
- REJECT;
- or RETURN FOR REVIEW.

Silence is not admission.

Implementation success is not admission.

Production readiness is not admission.

An AI recommendation is not admission.

---

# 7. Required Admission Evidence

Every successful Canonical Admission must conserve sufficient evidence to reconstruct:

- what candidate was examined;
- what immutable repository state was examined;
- the five-dimension examination;
- unresolved issues, if any;
- the Human Authority decision;
- the time/order of the decision;
- the canonical destination;
- the resulting canonical state;
- and the relationship to any previous canonical version.

Admission evidence must remain outside the canonical artifact itself or otherwise remain independently distinguishable from it.

The canonical artifact may reference its admission evidence.

The admission evidence must not rely solely on the canonical artifact to prove its own legitimacy.

---

# 8. Self-Legitimation Prohibition

The following are constitutionally invalid:

- a candidate declaring itself CANON;
- a script declaring its own output CANON without Human Authority;
- an AI treating its own recommendation as admission evidence;
- an audit converting its own unresolved REVIEW into PASS;
- a test redefining the admission requirement in order to pass;
- a file becoming CANON merely because it is copied into `canon/`;
- a commit message being treated as sufficient Human Canonical Admission;
- a software implementation granting itself canonical authority.

Authority cannot be self-generated.

---

# 9. Admission Failure

Canonical Admission must stop when:

- candidate identity is ambiguous;
- any mandatory dimension is GAP;
- any mandatory dimension is REVIEW;
- Human Authority is absent;
- Human decision is ambiguous;
- evidence does not correspond to the candidate examined;
- canonical destination is ambiguous;
- provenance is broken;
- or the candidate changed after examination.

Failure to admit does not imply rejection.

The candidate remains in its legitimate previous state unless Human Authority decides otherwise.

---

# 10. Canonical Mutation

Modification of existing Canon is not an ordinary file edit.

A canonical modification is a new governed admission event.

It must preserve:

- previous canonical identity;
- previous canonical content or immutable reference;
- proposed replacement;
- reason for change;
- relevant Evidence;
- Human Authority;
- resulting state;
- and historical relationship.

Previous Canon must not be erased from epistemic history.

---

# 11. Constitutional Bootstrap

CEP-000 is the first Human-Accepted constitutional candidate intended to bootstrap the governed canonical system.

Its Human Acceptance does not itself equal CANON.

CEP-000 must undergo the same five-dimension examination and Human Canonical Admission Gate defined here.

This mechanism may therefore govern the formal admission examination of CEP-000 without declaring CEP-000 admitted in advance.

The mechanism derives its procedural legitimacy from the already conserved Human Acceptance of the constitutional text, while canonical status remains pending until the explicit admission gate is completed.

---

# 12. PCC-01

PCC-01 is currently:

- IMPLEMENTED;
- PRODUCTION-READY;
- NOT CANON.

Implementation and Production-Ready evidence may contribute to Canonical Justification.

They do not substitute for Canonical Admission.

PCC-01 must undergo a separate canonical examination after the canonical governance foundation is operational.
