# AI-Toolkit Production Canon Contract

Version: 0.1.0

Status: Working Draft — Canon Extraction and Reconciliation

Classification: Production Governance / Canon-to-Implementation Contract

Human Authority: Owner

---

# 1. Purpose

The Production Canon Contract defines the controlled boundary between AI-Toolkit's accepted canonical and research-derived requirements and their implementation in the operational epistemic organism.

Its purpose is to prevent implementation from silently redefining, simplifying, bypassing, contradicting, or replacing the epistemic architecture established through canonical research and human authority.

The contract establishes the traceable path:

Canonical Basis
    ↓
Production Requirement
    ↓
Implementation
    ↓
Verification
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Human Acceptance where required

Implementation does not define Canon.

Code does not become authoritative merely because it exists.

AI does not gain canonical authority by implementing, interpreting, deriving, indexing, compressing, correlating, or reasoning over canonical material.

Where implementation and Canon disagree, the disagreement must become visible.

It must not be silently resolved by AI.

---

# 2. Human Sovereignty

AI-Toolkit shall contain no hidden epistemic authority.

Human authority extends across the entire epistemic organism.

AI may derive, organize, correlate, compress, index, transform, reason over, and temporarily represent information where required for operation.

Such representations shall not acquire independent canonical, governance, decision-making, or truth-defining authority.

Human authority must retain the legitimate ability to inspect, challenge, correct, reject, regenerate, supersede, or invalidate epistemically significant AI-derived representations.

Hidden implementation details may exist where technically necessary.

Hidden authority is prohibited.

A representation being normally invisible to the human interface does not remove it from human authority.

No internal layer may become an authority domain inaccessible to legitimate human inspection and control.

---

# 3. Canon-to-Production Rule

Production shall not be implemented from remembered research summaries alone.

For every epistemically significant production capability, the applicable saved Canon and accepted research material must be examined.

The required chain is:

Source
    ↓
Canonical / Research Status
    ↓
Production Requirement
    ↓
Implementation
    ↓
Test
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Traceability back to Source

An implementation agent may discover missing requirements.

It may not invent canonical answers to those missing requirements.

When a missing decision materially affects canonical meaning, the uncertainty must be exposed for human resolution.

---

# 4. Contract Interpretation

Every production requirement shall eventually contain the following elements.

## 4.1 Canonical Basis

The canonical or accepted research source from which the requirement originates.

## 4.2 Production Requirement

What the epistemic organism must actually do.

## 4.3 Prohibited Behaviour

What the implementation must not do.

## 4.4 Human Authority

Which decisions remain subject to human authority and which operations may be performed automatically under already-approved rules.

## 4.5 Required Provenance

What origin, contribution, transformation, lineage, evidence, and authority information must remain preserved.

## 4.6 Acceptance Evidence

What observable evidence demonstrates that the requirement has actually been realized.

## 4.7 Acceptance Tests

What verification must succeed before the capability can be represented as realized or operational.

## 4.8 Current Implementation Status

The evidence-bounded condition of the current implementation.

No implementation status may be stronger than the available evidence.

---

# 5. Reconciliation States

Every extracted requirement shall be classified before this contract becomes normative.

## CONFIRMED

Supported by applicable accepted canonical or research material and not currently known to conflict with a stronger applicable authority.

## RECONCILIATION REQUIRED

Relevant sources exist but conflict, overlap, or require a human decision before production interpretation is safe.

## RESEARCH ONLY

The concept exists in research but has not yet acquired sufficient authority to become a production requirement.

## SUPERSEDED

The requirement or interpretation belongs to an older state and has been replaced through a traceable later decision or authority.

## UNKNOWN

Available evidence is insufficient to establish its applicable status.

AI may not silently promote RESEARCH ONLY, RECONCILIATION REQUIRED, SUPERSEDED, or UNKNOWN material into CONFIRMED production Canon.

# 7. Human-Readable Epistemic Identity

Every epistemically significant entity shall possess both:

1. a stable machine-addressable identifier;
2. a concise human-readable semantic title.

Human-facing representations shall present both together whenever the semantic title is known.

An identifier establishes identity.

A title communicates meaning.

A human shall not be required to resolve opaque identifiers across repository artifacts merely to understand project history, state, decisions, evidence, or evolution.

Example:

NEED-0031 — Prevent Context Loss

rather than:

NEED-0031

This requirement applies to human-facing representations of:

- Needs;
- Decisions;
- Transformations;
- Experiences;
- Evidence;
- Witnesses;
- Memory;
- States;
- Lineage;
- canonical entities;
- other epistemically significant artifacts.

Machine-optimized derived representations may use compact identifiers where appropriate.

Such derived representations do not replace the human-readable authoritative representation.

---

# 8. No Naked Identifier Principle

In any human-facing representation, an epistemic identifier shall not be presented alone when its semantic title is known and can reasonably be resolved.

The human-readable representation should preserve:

IDENTIFIER — SEMANTIC TITLE

Machine-only derived representations may omit titles where this is justified by efficiency, provided that the semantic identity remains resolvable and human authority is not impaired.

# 11. Implementation Authority Boundary

Implementation agents may:

- inspect applicable sources;
- extract candidate requirements;
- implement CONFIRMED requirements;
- create tests;
- produce Evidence;
- report contradictions;
- report missing requirements;
- propose implementation alternatives;
- propose research questions;
- identify implementation gaps.

Implementation agents shall not:

- invent Canon;
- silently resolve canonical contradictions;
- promote research hypotheses into production authority;
- create hidden epistemic authority;
- erase original Experience because a summary exists;
- replace human-readable meaning with opaque machine identity in human-facing representations;
- represent unverified capability as operational;
- rewrite historical provenance to make the current architecture appear inevitable;
- silently reinterpret human purpose;
- claim knowledge where the available evidence establishes uncertainty;
- transform a derived internal representation into an independent authority.

When uncertainty materially affects canonical meaning:

STOP
    ↓
REPORT
    ↓
TRACE SOURCES
    ↓
REQUEST HUMAN AUTHORITY
    ↓
CONTINUE AFTER RESOLUTION

# 12. Evidence Rule

Production completion is not established merely by:

- code presence;
- filename presence;
- class presence;
- documentation presence;
- roadmap presence;
- AI assertion;
- generated output;
- historical claims of successful operation.

Production completion requires evidence appropriate to the claimed capability.

The Production Canon Contract shall specify the required evidence for each PCC block as reconciliation progresses.

Mention does not establish existence.

Implementation does not establish operation.

Historical verification does not automatically establish current verification after relevant Transformation.

Claims shall not exceed Evidence.

---

# 13. Original Experience Preservation

Sedimented Memory, summaries, Living Project Images, context packages, indexes, graphs, databases, vectors, and other derived representations must not silently replace the preserved original Experience from which they were derived.

Derived representations may improve accessibility and cognitive efficiency.

They do not retroactively become the original event.

Where the original Experience is legitimately retained, the system should preserve a resolvable path toward it.

Correction of later interpretation should not require falsification of earlier Experience.

---

# 14. Project-Owned Continuity

Project continuity shall not depend exclusively upon:

- one human's active memory;
- one AI context window;
- one ChatGPT conversation;
- one AI provider;
- one terminal session;
- one device;
- one temporary representation.

The project must progressively acquire the ability to preserve and expose its own governed continuity.

The external AI collaborator may change.

The project's epistemic continuity must remain.

---

# 15. Human Preservation Burden

The human should not be required to manually reconstruct project history that the epistemic organism could reasonably have captured during the original work.

The desired direction is:

capture during experience
    ↓
preserve
    ↓
organize
    ↓
sediment
    ↓
recall when necessary

rather than:

lose context
    ↓
ask the human to remember
    ↓
manually reconstruct
    ↓
hope the reconstruction is accurate

Human participation remains necessary for authority, meaning, correction, purpose, and decisions requiring human judgment.

Human participation should not be wasted recreating mechanically preservable history.

---

# 16. Capture Once, Reference Many

Where practical and compatible with integrity requirements, original Experience and Evidence should be preserved once and referenced from higher cognitive representations rather than copied repeatedly into every memory layer.

This principle exists to reduce:

- duplication;
- divergence;
- uncontrolled copies;
- contradictory historical representations;
- unnecessary information growth.

References must remain resolvable.

Efficiency shall not destroy auditability.

---

# 17. Evidence-Bounded Representation

Every representation of project reality must remain bounded by available Evidence.

A Living Project Image, Memory representation, context package, AI briefing, dashboard, or derived model must not represent uncertain or inferred information as established fact.

Relevant distinctions may include:

- verified;
- observed;
- inferred;
- proposed;
- accepted;
- rejected;
- superseded;
- unknown;
- contradictory;
- not currently verified.

The final vocabulary shall be reconciled with applicable Canon before normative use.
