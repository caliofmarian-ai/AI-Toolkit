AI-Toolkit `work/` workspace before consolidation, migration,

# 15. Classification Vocabulary

Every significant family or artifact in `work/` should eventually be
classified using evidence-backed human-readable states.

The following vocabulary is established for this audit process.

## HERITAGE

Inherited from an earlier architecture and potentially still valuable.

## CANONICAL CLAIM

The artifact claims canonical or comparable authority.

This classification records the claim.

It does not validate the claim.

## RECONSTRUCTION

Material created during an attempt to rebuild or redefine architecture.

## EXPERIMENT

Material produced to test or embody a concept without sufficient
evidence of mature production status.

## RESEARCH

Exploratory intellectual work not automatically possessing production
authority.

## EVIDENCE

Observed information, traces, audits, histories, outputs, witnesses or
other material used to establish what occurred.

## PROTOTYPE

A partial realization demonstrating some intended behavior.

## MATURE-ORGANISM CANDIDATE

Material potentially suitable for inheritance into the mature
epistemic organism.

## PCC INPUT

Material potentially relevant to a Production Canon Contract
requirement.

## SUPERSEDED

Material demonstrably replaced through a traceable later authority.

This state MUST NOT be assigned merely because something appears old.

## UNKNOWN

Available evidence is insufficient for a stronger classification.
