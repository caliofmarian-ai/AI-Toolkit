# Birth of Persistent Experience

## Research Context

This document materializes the research conversation that occurred immediately
before this transformation.

Problem discovered:

Information exchanged during the dialogue disappears if it is not
materialized immediately.

Observed consequences:

- Valuable discoveries remain only inside the ChatGPT conversation.
- Bash execution is preserved but the reasoning that generated it is lost.
- Long research sessions accumulate knowledge faster than it is preserved.
- Repository history becomes disconnected from the research history.

Research conclusions accepted by both participants:

1. Research must be materialized immediately.
2. Every Bash must preserve the research that generated it.
3. Bash alone is not sufficient.
4. Terminal output alone is not sufficient.
5. Dialogue is part of the experiment.
6. Bash is part of the experiment.
7. Terminal execution is part of the experiment.
8. Reflection is part of the experiment.
9. The resulting document must be self-contained.
