## 1. Why? — Need

PCC-02 established Transformation as the principal semantic unit of project evolution and provided temporal / causal Transformation lineage.

The organism still required an executable provenance anatomy capable of distinguishing where information originated, what was actually observed, what material constitutes Evidence, what Claim is being made, and whether that Claim has actually been verified.

Without this boundary, the organism could confuse sources, observations, evidence, interpretation, claims, and verification.
