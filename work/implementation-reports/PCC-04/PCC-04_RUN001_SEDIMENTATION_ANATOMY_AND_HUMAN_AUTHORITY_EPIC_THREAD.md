### Physiological need

The organism can already preserve Experience, understand meaningful
Transformation, maintain Provenance and Lineage, represent Verification,
promote governed Knowledge, and represent Current State.

The next canonical physiological need is Sedimentation:

the ability to represent that preserved epistemic reality has acquired a
candidate durable meaning without allowing interpretation to erase its origin
or allowing AI/software creation to impersonate Human Authority.

### RUN 001 implementation

RUN 001 introduces:

- Sedimentation;
- SedimentationTarget;
- SedimentationAuthority.

A Sedimentation contains:

- stable machine identity;
- human-readable semantic identity;
- explicit provenance identity;
- explicit proposed meaning;
- explicit proposed durable destination;
- explicit authority state;
- optional explicit uncertainty.

### Authority physiology

Every newly created Sedimentation begins as:

`PROPOSED`

Construction does not grant authority.

Software or AI cannot silently turn the proposal into accepted epistemic
reality.

Explicit Human Authority may produce an immutable accepted successor.

Explicit Human Authority may also produce an immutable rejected successor.

The original proposal remains unchanged.

### Provenance preservation

Sedimentation preserves an explicit provenance identifier.

The interpretation therefore retains a path toward the epistemic reality from
which it arose.

RUN 001 does not rewrite, delete, replace, or absorb the originating Experience
or Provenance.

### Epistemic honesty

Uncertainty may remain explicit.

Missing certainty is not automatically converted into certainty.

Proposal is not equivalent to accepted truth.

Accepted Sedimentation is not automatically instantiated as Memory or
Knowledge by RUN 001.
