# Epistemic Core

Purpose

The Core defines principles that every future capability must obey.

These principles are not implementation rules.

They are existence rules.

============================================================

CORE-001

Nothing exists without a Need.

============================================================

CORE-002

Nothing changes without a Transformation.

============================================================

CORE-003

Nothing is accepted without Evidence.

============================================================

CORE-004

Nothing becomes Knowledge until verified.

============================================================

CORE-005

Nothing becomes Memory until Knowledge is preserved.

============================================================

CORE-006

Identity is the continuity of preserved Memory.

============================================================

CORE-007

Capabilities are acquired.

They are never declared.

============================================================

CORE-008

Everything must be traceable back to its origin.

============================================================
