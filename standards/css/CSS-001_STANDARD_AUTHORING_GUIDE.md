# CSS-001 — Standard Authoring Guide

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

Classification: Canonical Standard

Standard Family: CSS

Identifier: CSS-001

Parent Standard: CSS-000_SPECIFICATION_MODEL

Owner: AI CTO

---

# Abstract

This specification defines the canonical process for authoring engineering standards within the AI-Toolkit ecosystem.

It establishes a deterministic, repeatable and auditable methodology for creating canonical standards that are suitable for both human authors and AI-driven generation.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this specification is to define the official engineering workflow for authoring canonical standards.

Every canonical standard shall follow this process.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification applies to:

- CDM
- CSL
- CSS
- CANON
- Governance Standards
- Future AI-generated standard families

---

# 3. Fundamental Principles

Every standard shall be:

- architecture-driven
- governance-controlled
- deterministic
- auditable
- traceable
- implementation-independent
- machine-readable
- extensible

---

# 4. Authoring Lifecycle

Every canonical standard shall follow the same lifecycle.

Need Identification

↓

Architecture Requirement (AR)

↓

Architecture Analysis

↓

Architecture Audit

↓

Architecture Decision Record (ADR)

↓

Architecture Approval

↓

Standard Family Definition

↓

Standard 000 Authoring

↓

Derived Standards Authoring

↓

Validation

↓

Technical Review

↓

Governance Approval

↓

Publication

↓

Maintenance

↓

Deprecation

↓

Archive

No phase may be skipped.

---

# 5. Preconditions

Before authoring begins, the following shall exist:

- Governance Foundation
- Architecture
- Architecture Audit
- Approved ADRs
- Repository Structure
- Standard Family Definition

---

# 6. Architecture First Principle

Architecture SHALL always precede implementation.

Architecture SHALL always precede standard authoring.

Standards SHALL never define architecture retroactively.

---

# 7. Audit Before Authoring

No canonical standard shall be written before:

architecture completeness audit

dependency audit

responsibility audit

gap analysis

have been completed.

---

# 8. Standard Structure

Every standard shall follow the Canonical Specification Model defined by CSS-000.

Mandatory sections shall not be omitted.

---

# 9. Normative Writing

Normative requirements shall use:

MUST

SHALL

SHOULD

MAY

MUST NOT

SHALL NOT

Informative language shall remain clearly distinguishable.

---

# 10. Traceability

Every engineering requirement shall be traceable.

Every normative statement shall be attributable.

Every evolution shall preserve history.

---

# 11. Versioning

Version evolution shall follow the governance process.

Breaking changes require explicit approval.

Backward compatibility shall be preserved whenever practical.

---

# 12. Review Process

Every standard shall undergo:

engineering review

architecture review

governance review

editorial review

validation review

Review evidence shall be retained.

---

# 13. Approval

Approval authority belongs to the canonical governance process.

Approval decisions shall be documented.

---

# 14. Publication

Only approved standards may become canonical.

Draft standards shall never replace approved standards.

Historical versions shall remain archived.

---

# 15. AI-generated Standards

Following completion of the foundational standards, future standard families shall normally be generated by the AI-Toolkit platform.

The platform shall:

analyze requirements

detect architectural gaps

propose architecture

generate audits

draft specifications

perform validation

generate traceability

prepare review material

AI-generated standards shall require governance approval before publication.

---

# 16. Human Responsibilities

Human architects remain responsible for:

governance

architecture approval

technical review

risk evaluation

final approval

AI assists engineering.

AI does not replace governance authority.

---

# 17. Quality Gates

Every standard shall satisfy:

Architecture Gate

Audit Gate

Authoring Gate

Validation Gate

Review Gate

Approval Gate

Publication Gate

Failure at any gate prevents progression.

---

# 18. Anti-patterns

The following practices are prohibited:

writing before architecture

implicit dependencies

undefined terminology

duplicate concepts

retroactive architecture

unapproved breaking changes

missing validation

missing traceability

---

# 19. Conformance

A canonical standard conforms to CSS-001 when it:

follows the prescribed lifecycle

passes every quality gate

conforms to CSS-000

maintains traceability

satisfies governance

---

# 20. Success Criteria

The Standard Authoring Guide is considered successfully implemented when every canonical standard is produced through a consistent, repeatable and auditable engineering process, regardless of whether it is authored by humans or generated by AI.

---

# 21. Closing Statement

The Standard Authoring Guide establishes a unified engineering workflow for the creation and evolution of canonical standards.

It ensures that every standard within AI-Toolkit is created through governance, architecture, validation and review, providing a stable foundation for AI-assisted engineering and long-term ecosystem evolution.
