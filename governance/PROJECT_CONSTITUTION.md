# AI-Toolkit Project Constitution

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

Classification: Canonical Governance Document

Owner: AI CTO

---

# Preamble

This Constitution establishes the governing principles of the AI-Toolkit ecosystem.

It defines the immutable rules under which the ecosystem is designed, governed, evolved and maintained.

All canonical standards, engineering processes, implementations and future extensions shall conform to this Constitution.

---

# Article I — Authority

This Constitution is the highest governance document of the AI-Toolkit project.

No canonical specification may contradict this document.

In case of conflict, this Constitution prevails.

---

# Article II — Canonical Authority

Canonical specifications constitute the authoritative definition of the system.

Source code is an implementation of canonical specifications.

Documentation is not descriptive; it is normative.

---

# Article III — Single Source of Truth

Every engineering concept shall have one canonical definition.

Duplicate definitions are prohibited.

Derived artifacts shall reference their canonical source.

---

# Article IV — Specification First

Engineering begins with canonical specifications.

Implementation shall never become the primary source of architectural knowledge.

---

# Article V — Architecture Before Implementation

Architectural decisions shall be approved before implementation begins.

Implementation shall follow architecture, not define it.

---

# Article VI — Traceability

Every canonical artifact shall be traceable.

Traceability shall include:

- origin
- dependencies
- implementation
- validation
- audit history
- lifecycle

---

# Article VII — Governance

All changes shall follow the official governance process:

Architecture Requirement

↓

Architecture Audit

↓

Architecture Decision Record

↓

Roadmap

↓

Canonical Standard

↓

Implementation

↓

Validation

↓

Release

---

# Article VIII — Standardization

Every engineering standard shall:

- have a defined scope
- avoid duplication
- define responsibilities
- expose measurable compliance criteria
- support future evolution

---

# Article IX — Quality

Quality shall be measurable.

Every canonical artifact shall define objective acceptance criteria.

Automated verification is preferred over manual verification.

---

# Article X — Security

Security shall be considered from the earliest design stages.

Every standard shall explicitly define its security implications.

---

# Article XI — Evolution

The ecosystem is designed for continuous evolution.

Backward compatibility shall be preserved whenever technically and economically reasonable.

Breaking changes require explicit governance approval.

---

# Article XII — Transparency

Architectural decisions shall be documented.

Engineering rationale shall be preserved.

Historical decisions shall remain traceable.

---

# Article XIII — Knowledge Preservation

Knowledge is a strategic asset.

Engineering knowledge shall remain independent from individual implementations, contributors or technologies.

---

# Article XIV — Canonical Models

The ecosystem is governed through canonical models.

Models define concepts.

Standards define rules.

Implementations realize standards.

---

# Article XV — Compliance

Compliance with canonical standards shall be evaluated through audits, validation rules and measurable engineering evidence.

---

# Article XVI — Amendments

This Constitution may evolve only through the official governance process.

Every amendment shall include:

- motivation
- impact analysis
- migration strategy
- approval record
- version history

---

# Article XVII — Final Declaration

The purpose of AI-Toolkit is not merely to produce software.

Its purpose is to establish a complete engineering discipline where knowledge, architecture, governance and implementation remain permanently aligned through canonical specifications.
