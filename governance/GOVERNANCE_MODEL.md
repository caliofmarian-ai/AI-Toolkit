# Introduction

This document defines the governance model of the AI-Toolkit ecosystem.

Governance establishes how engineering authority is distributed, how decisions are made, how canonical standards evolve and how engineering integrity is preserved throughout the lifecycle of the ecosystem.

---

# Purpose

The governance model ensures that architectural evolution remains:

- consistent
- traceable
- measurable
- auditable
- deterministic

Governance protects the integrity of canonical knowledge while allowing continuous evolution.

---

# Governance Objectives

The governance model shall:

- preserve canonical integrity
- eliminate uncontrolled architectural drift
- ensure transparent decision making
- support reproducible engineering
- guarantee traceability
- enable automated auditing

---

Reviewers

Implementers

Each role has clearly defined responsibilities.

---

# Responsibilities

Project Owner

- defines strategic direction
- approves major architectural evolution
- approves governance changes

AI CTO

- maintains canonical standards
- coordinates architectural evolution
- validates engineering consistency
- supervises canonical models

Architecture Board

- evaluates architectural proposals
- reviews ADRs
- approves structural changes

Engineering Contributors

- propose improvements
- implement approved work
- maintain engineering quality

Auditors

- evaluate compliance
- verify traceability
- produce engineering evidence

Automation Systems

- execute validation
- perform audits
- verify conformance
- generate reports

Strategic decisions require governance approval.

Engineering decisions shall remain aligned with canonical standards.

Implementation decisions shall not contradict architecture.

# Closing Statement

The AI-Toolkit Governance Model establishes a structured, transparent and deterministic framework for engineering decision making.

Its objective is not to restrict innovation, but to ensure that innovation remains consistent with canonical knowledge, architectural integrity and long-term sustainability.
