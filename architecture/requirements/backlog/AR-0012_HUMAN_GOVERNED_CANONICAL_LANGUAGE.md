# AR-0012 — Human Governed Canonical Language

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Proposed

Classification: Architecture Requirement

Owner: AI CTO

---

# Purpose

Establish the principle that the canonical engineering language of AI-Toolkit shall remain permanently governed by human-readable specifications.

---

# Motivation

AI systems may internally transform information for processing.

However, the canonical engineering language shall always remain understandable, reviewable and controllable by humans.

---

# Core Principles

The following principles are proposed.

## Human Authority

Human-readable canonical specifications are the single authoritative source.

---

## Human Readability

Every canonical specification shall remain understandable by a human engineer.

---

## No Hidden Canon

No internal AI representation may become the canonical engineering source.

---

## Explainability

Every engineering conclusion produced by AI shall be traceable to canonical specifications.

---

## Human Override

Human authorities shall always be able to review, reject or override AI-generated engineering decisions.

---

## Transparency

Canonical engineering decisions shall remain explainable.

---

## Reproducibility

Identical canonical inputs shall produce reproducible engineering outcomes under the same approved system version.

---

# Expected Benefits

The proposed principles improve:

- transparency;
- governance;
- auditability;
- explainability;
- long-term human control.
