# Governance Foundation Audit

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

Classification: Canonical Architecture Audit

---

# Purpose

This audit evaluates the consistency, completeness and architectural integrity of the Governance Foundation before the Canonical Standards are authored.

The objective is to verify that the governance layer provides a stable basis for CDM, CSL, CANON and every future engineering standard.
