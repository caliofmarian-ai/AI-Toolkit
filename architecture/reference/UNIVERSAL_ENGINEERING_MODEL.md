# Purpose

This document defines the highest-level architectural model of AI-Toolkit.

Its purpose is to explain how every major component of the platform relates to every other component.

This document is descriptive rather than implementation-specific.

It serves as the conceptual map of the entire AI-Toolkit ecosystem.

# Layer Definitions

## Human

The Human is the highest engineering authority.

Only the Human may approve changes to Canonical Knowledge.

---

## Governance

Governance defines authority, ownership, approval and engineering rules.

It determines who may change knowledge and under which conditions.

---

## Canonical Knowledge

Canonical Knowledge is the authoritative engineering knowledge of the platform.

All implementation derives from Canonical Knowledge.

Implementation never becomes authoritative by itself.

---

## CSS

Defines how canonical standards are written.

---

## CDM

Defines how engineering information is represented.

---

## CSL

Defines how engineering knowledge is formally expressed.

---

## Engineering Models

Engineering Models transform canonical specifications into executable representations.

Examples include:

- Object Models
- Knowledge Models
- Dependency Models
- Semantic Models
- Graph Models
- Reasoning Models

---

## Engineering Engines

Engineering Engines execute the Engineering Models.

Examples include:

- Knowledge Engine
- Repository Engine
- Validation Engine
- Planning Engine
- Executive Engine
- Reasoning Engine

---

## Runtime

The Runtime coordinates every Engineering Engine.

It provides continuous execution, scheduling, orchestration and monitoring.

---

## AI CTO

The AI CTO is the primary operational intelligence built on top of the Runtime.

It consumes Engineering Engines and Canonical Knowledge to assist software engineering.

---

## Dashboard

The Dashboard is the operational interface of the Runtime.

It visualizes Runtime state.

It does not define engineering logic.

---

# AI-Toolkit

AI-Toolkit is the complete engineering ecosystem.

It includes:

- Governance
- Canonical Knowledge
- CSS
- CDM
- CSL
- Engineering Models
- Engineering Engines
- Runtime
- AI CTO
- Dashboard
- Integrations
- Future AI Products

---

# Design Principles

Implementation SHALL derive from Canonical Knowledge.

Runtime SHALL execute Engineering Engines.

Dashboard SHALL represent Runtime state.

Human Authority SHALL remain the final authority.

Every engineering decision SHALL remain traceable.

Every engineering artifact SHALL preserve provenance.

---

# Future Evolution

The Universal Engineering Model allows additional AI systems to be created using the same foundation.

Examples include:

- AI CTO
- AI Architect
- AI Project Manager
- AI QA Director
- AI Security Officer
- AI Business Analyst

All future systems SHALL reuse the same Canonical Foundation.
