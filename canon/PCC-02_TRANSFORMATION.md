## Canonical Identity

PCC-02 is the **Transformation** organ of the AI-Toolkit Epistemic Organism.

A Transformation is the persistent epistemic representation of meaningful
project evolution.

It is not merely a Git commit, a code diff, a task, a conversation, or a
runtime event.

It preserves the intelligible evolutionary unit through which the organism can
understand what changed, why it changed, what happened, what was learned, and
what transformation may follow.

---

## Canonical Purpose

PCC-02 exists so meaningful project evolution can survive beyond the execution
or conversation that produced it.

Transformation must remain:

- persistent;
- reconstructable;
- epistemically explicit;
- human-readable;
- machine-identifiable;
- causally navigable;
- relational;
- inspectable;
- honest about unknown reality.

---

## Canonical Transformation Anatomy

A complete Transformation preserves these twelve epistemic dimensions:

1. **Need**
2. **Research**
3. **Hypothesis**
4. **Owner Decision**
5. **Implementation**
6. **Execution**
7. **Artifacts / Effects**
8. **Evidence**
9. **Verification**
10. **Knowledge**
11. **Evolution**

These dimensions form the canonical semantic anatomy of PCC-02.

Implementation mechanisms may evolve, but an implementation claiming

---

## Canonical Unknown-State Rule

PCC-02 must never invent knowledge merely to make a Transformation appear
complete.

Unknown information remains explicitly unknown.

Work not demonstrated as executed must not be represented as executed.

Work not demonstrated as verified must not be represented as verified.

Missing or unresolved related reality must remain explicitly unresolved.

Epistemic honesty has priority over apparent completeness.

## Canonical Identity

Transformation requires both machine continuity and human meaning.

The current implementation uses the stable `TR-*` identity family.

The machine identifier is not itself the semantic meaning of the
Transformation.

Human-readable identity derives from the Transformation's meaningful semantic
content rather than from a second competing source of truth.

Identity must survive persistence and reconstruction.

---

## Canonical Persistence and Reconstruction

A Transformation must survive process or lifecycle restart.

Persisted Transformation reality must be reconstructable without silently
inventing missing state.

The current executable organ reconstructs Transformation through its
established recovery interface.

The concrete storage and API mechanism may evolve under Canon provided the
canonical semantic guarantees remain intact.

---

## Canonical Temporal and Causal Continuity

Transformation may preserve causal relationship to prior Transformation.

The organism must be capable of distinguishing and navigating relevant
Transformation continuity without falsifying unavailable ancestry.

Local history may be enumerated.

Direct descendants may be navigated.

Available lineage may be reconstructed.

Unavailable ancestry must not be invented.

Cycles that contradict valid causal lineage must not be accepted as legitimate
history.

---

## Canonical Relations

A Transformation may preserve explicit relations to other epistemic reality.

A relation must preserve sufficient meaning to identify what kind of relation
exists and what reality it targets.

A Transformation must not copy an external epistemic organ merely to represent
a relation to it.

The existence of a relation does not confer ownership of the related organ.

---

## Canonical Navigability and Inspection

Transformation reality must be inspectable.

Where a related manifestation is locally resolvable, the organism may expose
that manifestation.

Where reality cannot be resolved, the unresolved state must remain explicit.

Identity must not be confused with filesystem location.

Inspection must not create a second persistence authority.

---

## Canonical Boundary with PCC-01

PCC-01 Persistent Experience and PCC-02 Transformation are complementary
organs.

Persistent Experience preserves continuity of experience.

Transformation preserves meaningful project evolution.

PCC-02 must remain compatible with inherited PCC-01 behavior and must not
silently replace the Experience organ.

---

## Canonical Boundary with PCC-03

PCC-03 — Provenance + Lineage remains a separate epistemic organ.

PCC-02 may preserve causal Transformation continuity and explicit relations
necessary to understand a Transformation.

This does not make PCC-02 the complete Provenance organ.

Broader intellectual origin, contribution semantics, attribution, and
provenance evolution belong to PCC-03.
