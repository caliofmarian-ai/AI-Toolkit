# Engineering Audit Engine Specification

Version: 5.0.0

Status: Draft

Classification: Canonical Specification

Authoritative Source: AI CTO Operating System

---

# Purpose

This specification defines the Engineering Audit Engine (EAE), the canonical subsystem responsible for continuous engineering auditing, evidence collection, compliance verification, report generation and engineering recommendations.

The Engineering Audit Engine SHALL become the authoritative verification subsystem of AI CTO Operating System.

Every engineering subsystem SHALL be auditable.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Engineering Audit Engine is to continuously verify that the implementation, architecture, repositories and engineering knowledge remain compliant with canonical specifications.

Auditing SHALL become a continuous engineering activity rather than a manual process.

---

# Vision

Engineering audits SHALL become executable.

Audit specifications SHALL describe engineering intent.

The Engineering Audit Engine SHALL transform audit specifications into continuously executed engineering verification.

Manual audit reports SHALL become exceptions.

Generated audit reports SHALL become the engineering source of truth.

---

# Scope

The Engineering Audit Engine governs:

canonical audits

repository audits

architecture audits

report generation

recommendation generation

# Core Principles

## Continuous Auditing

Engineering audits SHALL execute continuously.

---

## Evidence Based

Every finding SHALL include engineering evidence.

---

## Explainable Findings

Every engineering finding SHALL preserve its reasoning.

---

## Deterministic Results

Equivalent engineering inputs SHALL produce equivalent audit findings.

---

## Automatic Reporting

Engineering reports SHALL be generated automatically.

---

## Historical Comparison

Every audit SHALL compare current results with previous executions.
