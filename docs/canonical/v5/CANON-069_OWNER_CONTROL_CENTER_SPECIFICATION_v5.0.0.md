# CANON-069

# Owner Control Center Specification

Version: 5.0.0

Status: Draft

Classification: Canonical Specification

Authoritative Source: AI CTO Operating System

---

# Purpose

This specification defines the Owner Control Center (OCC), the canonical command interface through which the Owner governs the AI CTO Operating System.

The Owner Control Center SHALL become the single authoritative interface for strategic oversight, operational control, approvals, monitoring, and interaction with AI CTO.

No subsystem SHALL bypass Owner Control Center for operations requiring Owner authorization.

---

# Mission

The mission of Owner Control Center is to establish a permanent, secure, and transparent relationship between the Owner and AI CTO.

The Owner SHALL remain the highest authority.

AI CTO SHALL remain the executive engineering system.

Owner Control Center is the bridge between them.

---

# Vision

Owner Control Center is not merely a dashboard.

It is the executive headquarters of AI CTO.

From a single interface the Owner SHALL be capable of:

- understanding the complete engineering state
- monitoring every repository
- supervising autonomous execution
- approving strategic decisions
- reviewing engineering recommendations
- managing AI CTO itself

---

# Scope

Owner Control Center SHALL govern:

- repositories
- workspaces
- engineering memory
- canonical specifications
- RFCs
- execution engines
- runtime services
- autonomous agents
- engineering reports
- approvals
- security policies
- system configuration

Every strategic engineering activity SHALL be observable.

---

# Core Principles

## Owner Sovereignty

The Owner is the final decision authority.

AI CTO SHALL never replace the Owner.

AI CTO SHALL assist the Owner.

---

## Complete Transparency

Every engineering activity SHALL be visible.

Nothing important happens silently.

Every action is observable.

Every recommendation is traceable.

---

## Explainability

Every recommendation SHALL include:

- reasoning
- evidence
- confidence
- affected repositories
- canonical references

The Owner SHALL understand why AI CTO recommends an action.

---

## Controlled Autonomy

Autonomous execution SHALL always respect governance policies.

Owner approval SHALL be mandatory whenever required by canonical policy.

---

## Persistent Relationship

Owner identity SHALL remain persistent across runtime sessions.

AI CTO SHALL continuously improve its understanding of Owner engineering preferences.
