# Engineering Audit Framework Specification

Version: 5.0.0

Status: Draft

Classification: Canonical Specification

Authoritative Source: AI CTO Operating System

---

# Purpose

This specification defines the Engineering Audit Framework (EAF), the canonical framework governing every engineering audit executed within AI CTO Operating System.

The Engineering Audit Framework SHALL standardize audit definition, execution, evidence collection, scoring, reporting, historical comparison and recommendation generation.

Every engineering audit SHALL conform to this specification.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Engineering Audit Framework is to provide one unified auditing architecture capable of continuously verifying engineering quality across the complete AI CTO Operating System.

Auditing SHALL become deterministic, repeatable and fully automated.

---

# Vision

Engineering audits SHALL no longer be static documents.

Instead, engineering audits SHALL become executable engineering specifications.

Each audit SHALL be capable of producing engineering evidence, engineering scores, engineering recommendations and engineering history without manual report writing.
