# Purpose

This specification defines the Engineering Maturity Engine (EME), the canonical subsystem responsible for continuously measuring, evaluating and improving the engineering maturity of AI CTO Operating System.

The Engineering Maturity Engine SHALL provide objective measurements describing the current engineering capability, implementation completeness and long-term evolution of the platform.

Engineering maturity SHALL become a measurable engineering asset.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Engineering Maturity Engine is to transform engineering progress into measurable maturity indicators.

Engineering maturity SHALL continuously improve through evidence, auditing and learning.

---

# Vision

Engineering maturity SHALL never rely upon subjective opinion.

Every maturity assessment SHALL be supported by engineering evidence generated by the Engineering Audit Engine.

Engineering maturity SHALL become observable, measurable, explainable and continuously improvable.

The Engineering Maturity Engine SHALL evaluate engineering capability using a canonical maturity model.

---

# Engineering Maturity Levels

The canonical engineering maturity levels are:

Level 0

Concept

The capability exists only as an engineering idea.

---

Level 1

Specified

Canonical specifications have been completed.

Architecture exists.

Implementation has not yet begun.

---

Level 2

Implemented

Core implementation exists.

Major engineering capabilities remain incomplete.

Validation is partial.

---

Level 3

Verified

Implementation satisfies canonical requirements.

Validation passes.

Engineering evidence exists.

Audit findings remain acceptable.

---

Level 4

Operational

Engineering capability operates continuously.

Observability is complete.

Recommendations are produced automatically.

Engineering workflows are stable.

---

Level 5

Optimizing

Engineering capability continuously improves itself using engineering evidence.

Recommendations become increasingly accurate.

Learning continuously improves engineering quality.
