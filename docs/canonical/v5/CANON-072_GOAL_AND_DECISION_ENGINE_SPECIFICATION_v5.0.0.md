# Purpose

This specification defines the Goal and Decision Engine (GDE), the canonical subsystem responsible for engineering objectives, prioritization, decision support and execution intent.

The Goal and Decision Engine SHALL transform engineering awareness into actionable engineering direction.

It SHALL become the strategic planning core of AI CTO Operating System.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Goal and Decision Engine is to ensure that every engineering action performed by AI CTO is intentional, justified, prioritized and aligned with Owner objectives and canonical governance.

AI CTO SHALL never perform arbitrary engineering work.

Every action SHALL originate from an explicit engineering goal.

---

# Vision

The Goal and Decision Engine is not a task manager.

It is the strategic reasoning system responsible for deciding:

What should be done?

Why should it be done?

When should it be done?

What depends on it?

Who must approve it?

What engineering value will it produce?

# Decision Levels

Every engineering decision SHALL belong to one of four levels.

Level 1

Autonomous

Safe engineering decisions requiring no Owner approval.

---

Level 2

Recommended

AI CTO recommends.

Owner MAY review.

---

Level 3

Approval Required

Owner authorization is mandatory.

---

Level 4

Restricted

AI CTO SHALL NOT perform the operation.

Only the Owner may authorize exceptional execution.
