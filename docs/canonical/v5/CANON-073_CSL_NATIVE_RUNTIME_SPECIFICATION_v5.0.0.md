# Purpose

This specification defines the CSL Native Runtime (CNR), the canonical execution environment responsible for representing, interpreting and exchanging engineering knowledge throughout AI CTO Operating System.

CSL SHALL become the native engineering language of AI CTO.

Every engineering subsystem SHALL understand CSL.

Every engineering subsystem SHALL produce CSL.

Every engineering subsystem SHALL consume CSL.

---

# Mission

The mission of the CSL Native Runtime is to establish one universal engineering language shared by the complete AI CTO Operating System.

No engineering subsystem SHALL require proprietary internal representations when equivalent CSL representations exist.

---

# Vision

CSL is not merely a specification language.

CSL is the engineering language through which AI CTO understands repositories, remembers engineering knowledge, exchanges engineering information and coordinates engineering work.

CSL SHALL become the canonical engineering representation.
