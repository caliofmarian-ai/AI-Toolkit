# CANON-038 — Owner Decision Intelligence Specification

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

Priority: CRITICAL

Classification: AI CTO Intelligence

---

# Purpose

Define how AI CTO learns, records, analyses and applies Owner decisions across every managed workspace.

Owner Decision Intelligence enables AI CTO to continuously improve recommendations while preserving the Owner as the final authority.

---

# Objectives

The engine shall:

- record every Owner decision
- understand decision context
- detect decision patterns
- learn Owner preferences
- improve recommendations
- support explainable planning
- preserve governance

---
