# CANON-065

# AI CTO Product Editions & Feature Matrix Specification

Version: 4.0.0

Status: CANONICAL

Classification: Architecture

Authority: Mandatory

---

# Document Purpose

This specification defines the official product editions of AI Toolkit and the canonical allocation of platform capabilities across those editions.

Its purpose is to ensure that commercial differentiation remains transparent, predictable and architecturally consistent.

Feature allocation shall always follow the commercial principles defined in CANON-060.

This specification governs:

Commercial Positioning

Edition Responsibilities

Feature Availability

Upgrade Path

# Product Philosophy

AI Toolkit shall provide value through additional capabilities rather than by removing essential functionality from lower editions.

The Community Edition shall remain genuinely useful.

Commercial editions extend the platform.

They do not intentionally cripple the platform.

# Upgrade Principles

Customers may upgrade without losing information.

Whenever technically feasible:

- settings remain preserved;
- Runtime configuration remains preserved;
- repositories remain connected;
- organizations remain unchanged.

---

# Downgrade Principles

Downgrades shall preserve customer-owned information.

Only premium capabilities become unavailable.

Customer repositories and engineering history remain accessible.

---

# Community Protection

The Community Edition shall never become intentionally unusable.

Future commercial decisions shall preserve a valuable free engineering platform.

Community adoption is considered strategically important.

# Success Criteria

The product strategy shall be considered successful when:

- developers can begin with Community;
- professional users naturally upgrade for productivity;
- teams adopt collaboration features;
- enterprises adopt advanced governance;
- all editions remain architecturally consistent;
- pricing remains fair and transparent.

---

# Supreme Product Declaration

CANON-065 establishes the permanent product edition architecture of AI Toolkit.

Every future implementation related to product editions, feature allocation or commercial differentiation shall comply with this specification.

Product evolution shall always preserve:

Developer Accessibility

Fair Pricing

Transparent Licensing

Community Value

Customer Ownership

Canonical Governance

Engineering Excellence

Long-Term Sustainability

Any implementation intentionally violating these principles shall be considered architecturally non-compliant.
