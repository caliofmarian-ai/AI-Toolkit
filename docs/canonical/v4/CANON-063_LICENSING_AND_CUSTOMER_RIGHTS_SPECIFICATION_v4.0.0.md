# CANON-063

# AI CTO Licensing & Customer Rights Specification

Version: 4.0.0

Status: CANONICAL

Classification: Architecture

Authority: Mandatory

---

# Document Purpose

This specification defines the canonical licensing model and customer rights for AI Toolkit.

Its objective is to ensure that licensing remains transparent, fair, developer-friendly and legally sustainable while protecting both the platform and its customers.

Licensing shall encourage long-term trust rather than create artificial dependency.

---

# Scope

This specification governs:

Licensing

Customer Rights

Customer Responsibilities

Data Ownership

Intellectual Property

Commercial Fairness

Export Rights

Platform Responsibilities

License Compliance

Future Licensing Evolution

# Licensing Philosophy

Licensing exists to define rights and responsibilities.

Licensing shall never become a mechanism that intentionally restricts customer freedom.

Customers remain partners of the platform.

Trust shall always be preferred over artificial lock-in.

---

# Core Licensing Principles

Licensing shall preserve:

Transparency

Fairness

Developer Accessibility

Privacy

Customer Ownership

Commercial Sustainability

Canonical Governance

Long-Term Trust

---

# Customer Ownership

Customers always own:

their repositories;

their source code;

their documentation;

their engineering history;

their runtime configuration;

their exported reports;

their engineering knowledge generated from their own projects.

AI Toolkit never claims ownership of customer intellectual property.

---

# AI Toolkit Ownership

AI Toolkit retains ownership of:

platform source code;

runtime architecture;

canonical documentation;

platform trademarks;

branding;

commercial services;

hosted infrastructure.

Ownership of the platform never transfers ownership of customer projects.

---

# Intellectual Property

Customer software remains the intellectual property of the customer unless contractual agreements explicitly state otherwise.

Platform improvements remain the intellectual property of AI Toolkit unless released under separate licensing terms.

---

# Customer Rights

Every customer possesses the following permanent rights.

---

## Right to Access

Customers may access their subscribed services according to their active plan.

---

## Right to Export

Customers may export:

repositories;

runtime reports;

configuration;

engineering history;

documentation;

generated reports;

knowledge created from their own projects.

Exports shall use documented formats whenever practical.

---

## Right to Leave

Customers may discontinue using AI Toolkit at any time.

The platform shall not intentionally create technical barriers that prevent migration.

---

## Right to Data Ownership

Customer-owned information remains owned by the customer throughout the entire commercial lifecycle.

---

## Right to Privacy

Customer information shall be processed only for legitimate operational purposes.

---

## Right to Transparency

Customers shall understand:

license terms;

subscription limits;

pricing;

commercial policies;

data processing;

support policies.

Documentation shall remain publicly accessible whenever possible.

---

## Right to Fair Treatment

Equivalent subscriptions shall receive equivalent commercial treatment.

Commercial discrimination without legitimate justification is prohibited.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

Customers shall:

protect credentials;

respect applicable laws;

respect intellectual property;

avoid abuse of platform resources;

comply with published commercial policies.

---

# Platform Responsibilities

AI Toolkit shall:

protect customer information;

maintain commercially reasonable availability;

provide documented services;

respect customer ownership;

maintain transparent commercial policies;

provide export capabilities;

preserve engineering integrity.

---

# No Vendor Lock-in

AI Toolkit shall never intentionally prevent customers from:

exporting their own information;

moving to another platform;

continuing development elsewhere.

Customer retention shall be earned through platform quality.

---

# Data Portability

Portable exports shall be supported whenever technically feasible.

Examples include:

JSON

Markdown

CSV

PDF

Open formats are preferred over proprietary formats.

---

# Subscription Independence

License ownership and customer-owned engineering data remain separate concerns.

Subscription expiration shall not transfer ownership of customer information.

---

# License Compliance

Customers shall use the platform according to the published licensing terms.

Commercial misuse may result in service restrictions consistent with documented policies.

---

# Educational Use

AI Toolkit encourages educational usage.

Future educational licensing programs may be introduced.

---

# Research Use

Academic and non-commercial research usage may receive dedicated licensing programs through future commercial policies.

---

# Open Source Relationship

AI Toolkit may include Community components.

Commercial editions extend the platform with hosted services and advanced capabilities.

Commercial success shall strengthen the Community Edition rather than replace it.

---

# Future Licensing Evolution

Future licensing capabilities may include:

Academic Licenses

Startup Programs

Partner Programs

Marketplace Licensing

Enterprise Contracts

OEM Licensing

Government Programs

Open Research Programs

Future evolution shall preserve the principles established by CANON-063.

---

# Supreme Licensing Declaration

CANON-063 establishes the permanent licensing philosophy of AI Toolkit.

Every future implementation related to licensing, customer rights, commercial usage or intellectual property shall comply with this specification.

Licensing shall always preserve:

Customer Ownership

Developer Freedom

Fair Commercial Practice

Transparent Licensing

No Vendor Lock-in

Privacy by Design

Canonical Governance

Long-Term Sustainability

Any implementation intentionally violating these principles shall be considered architecturally non-compliant.
