# CANON-060

# AI CTO Commercial Platform Specification

Version: 4.0.0

Status: CANONICAL

Classification: Architecture

Authority: Mandatory

---

# Document Purpose

This specification defines the commercial architecture of the AI Toolkit Platform.

Its purpose is not merely to define subscriptions or billing.

Its purpose is to establish the permanent commercial principles that shall govern every future commercial capability of AI Toolkit.

Commercial implementation shall always remain subordinate to the architectural principles defined by the canonical specifications.

Revenue generation shall never compromise engineering integrity, transparency or user ownership.

# Commercial Philosophy

AI Toolkit is designed to become a sustainable commercial platform without sacrificing openness, engineering quality or user trust.

Commercial success shall be achieved by providing value rather than restricting users.

The platform shall compete through engineering excellence instead of artificial limitations.

Commercial architecture shall strengthen the platform rather than reduce user freedom.

---

# Core Commercial Principles

Every future commercial implementation shall comply with the following principles.

---

## Principle 1

User First

The interests of legitimate users shall remain the highest commercial priority.

Commercial decisions shall improve the user experience instead of creating unnecessary obstacles.

---

## Principle 2

Developer First

Independent developers shall always remain an important part of the AI Toolkit ecosystem.

The platform shall remain affordable for individual developers, students, researchers and small startups.

---

## Principle 3

Engineering First

Engineering quality shall always take priority over short-term commercial gain.

No commercial implementation shall intentionally reduce software quality.

---

## Principle 4

Trust Before Revenue

Long-term trust is considered more valuable than short-term revenue.

Commercial architecture shall encourage long-term relationships rather than maximize immediate profit.

---

## Principle 5

Transparency

Pricing, licensing and subscriptions shall remain clear, understandable and publicly documented.

Hidden limitations are prohibited.

---

## Principle 6

Fairness

Commercial rules shall apply consistently to every customer.

Pricing shall remain reasonable for the value delivered.

---

## Principle 7

Long-Term Sustainability

Commercial success exists to ensure continuous development of AI Toolkit.

Revenue supports innovation, maintenance, infrastructure and long-term architectural evolution.

---

# Commercial Values

The commercial identity of AI Toolkit is founded upon enduring values rather than temporary business strategies.

These values shall remain valid throughout the lifetime of the platform.

---

## User Ownership

Users always own:

- their repositories;
- their source code;
- their documentation;
- their engineering history;
- their exported reports;
- their engineering knowledge.

AI Toolkit never claims ownership over customer software.

---

## No Vendor Lock-in

The platform shall never intentionally prevent users from leaving.

Customers shall always be able to export their own information.

Commercial architecture shall encourage users to remain because the platform provides value, not because leaving is difficult.

---

## Privacy by Design

Privacy shall be integrated into the platform architecture.

Only the minimum required information shall be processed.

Commercial features shall never require unnecessary collection of personal information.

---

## Cloud Pays for Cloud

Customers pay for hosted services.

They do not pay for ownership of their own engineering work.

Examples of premium services include:

- continuous Runtime hosting;
- cloud infrastructure;
- enterprise collaboration;
- hosted Engineering Agents;
- operational monitoring;
- managed integrations;
- premium support.

---

## Community First

The Community Edition shall remain useful.

It shall not become intentionally crippled to force subscription upgrades.

Commercial editions shall extend capabilities rather than remove existing functionality.

---

## Accessible Pricing

Pricing should remain accessible.

AI Toolkit is intended to support:

- students;
- independent developers;
- open-source maintainers;
- startups;
- small businesses;
- enterprise organizations.

Affordable entry points are considered a strategic objective.

# Customer Rights

Every customer shall retain the following rights.

---

## Right to Data Ownership

Customers own their engineering information.

AI Toolkit does not acquire ownership of customer projects.

---

## Right to Export

Customers may export their own:

repositories

documentation

engineering reports

runtime reports

configuration

knowledge generated from their own projects

Export functionality shall remain available regardless of subscription changes.

---

## Right to Downgrade

Customers may downgrade their subscription.

Downgrading shall never intentionally destroy customer information.

Premium functionality may become unavailable, but customer-owned data shall remain accessible for export.

---

## Right to Transparency

Customers have the right to understand:

pricing

license terms

subscription conditions

data processing

platform responsibilities

commercial limitations

These shall be documented clearly.

---

## Right to Fair Treatment

Commercial decisions shall not discriminate unfairly between customers.

Equivalent subscriptions shall receive equivalent services.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

protecting their credentials

complying with applicable laws

respecting software licenses

respecting intellectual property

using the platform responsibly

Commercial misuse may result in service limitations according to published policies.

# Subscription Principles

Subscriptions shall fund the continuous development of AI Toolkit while preserving customer trust.

Subscriptions are intended to purchase services, infrastructure and additional capabilities.

Subscriptions do not transfer ownership of customer software to AI Toolkit.

# Grace Period

A reasonable grace period shall exist after subscription expiration.

During this period the customer shall be able to:

renew the subscription;

export data;

review reports;

recover billing issues.

Grace periods improve customer experience and reduce accidental service interruptions.

---

# Subscription Expiration

Subscription expiration shall never intentionally prevent customers from accessing their own engineering information.

After expiration:

premium cloud capabilities may stop;

premium support may end;

enterprise functionality may become unavailable;

customer-owned data remains accessible for export according to the published retention policy.

---

# Upgrade Policy

Customers may upgrade at any time.

Upgrades shall activate without unnecessary interruption whenever technically feasible.

---

# Downgrade Policy

Customers may downgrade voluntarily.

Downgrades shall preserve customer-owned information.

Only premium functionality may be removed.

---

# Cancellation Policy

Customers may cancel subscriptions without hidden penalties.

Cancellation procedures shall remain transparent.

Commercial trust is considered more valuable than forcing customer retention.

---

# Commercial Success Definition

AI Toolkit shall be considered commercially successful when:

customers voluntarily remain on the platform because it provides exceptional engineering value;

pricing remains fair and transparent;

the Community Edition remains healthy;

commercial revenue sustainably finances long-term development;

the platform maintains user trust while continuing to innovate.

Commercial success shall never be achieved by reducing user freedom or restricting ownership of customer data.

CANON-060 establishes the permanent commercial philosophy of AI Toolkit.

engineering excellence;

user ownership;

transparent licensing;

fair pricing;

privacy by design;

Any future commercial implementation that intentionally violates these principles shall be considered architecturally non-compliant.
